About

Austen is an open source web app that helps you discover and share the relationships between characters in novels. It shows a visual map of how characters are connected, using Mermaid syntax generated by Google Gemini. You can also share the generated graphs with your friends.

Stacks

  • Angular
  • Analog
  • RxJS
  • Supabase
  • Mermaid
  • TypeScript
  • Google Generative AI
